1. Problem Statement
Air pollution is a growing environmental challenge. People often lack accessible information about the quality of air they breathe, pollution hotspots, changing pollution levels and appropriate precautions.
Your task is to build a Real-Time Air Quality Intelligence Platform using authentic public APIs to monitor, visualize and analyze air pollution.
The platform should help people understand air quality, explore pollution levels across different locations, track historical changes and make informed decisions about outdoor activities.
The goal is to turn raw environmental data into meaningful, accessible and actionable insights.
2. API Integration (Mandatory)
Integrate at least one authentic, publicly available air quality API to fetch real-world data.
Requirements:
* Fetch real-world air quality measurements.
* Display AQI and available pollutant concentrations.
* Show the data source and last updated timestamp.
* Handle API failures, missing data and rate limits.
* Secure API credentials using environment variables.
* Clearly distinguish actual measurements from forecasts and estimates.
Important: Hardcoded or fabricated AQI values must not be presented as live data.
3. Mandatory Features
A. Live AQI Dashboard
* Display real-time AQI.
* Show PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, CO and O3 wherever available.
* Display pollution severity categories.
* Show last updated timestamp.
* Provide an easy-to-understand dashboard.
B. Interactive Air Quality Map
* Display pollution levels on an interactive map.
* Use color-coded AQI indicators.
* Allow users to explore different locations.
* Show detailed AQI information when selecting a location.
* Highlight pollution hotspots.
C. Historical Pollution Tracker
* Compare air quality across different dates.
* Display historical AQI charts.
* Compare PM2.5 and PM10 levels.
* Show daily pollution trends.
* Calculate percentage increases or decreases.
* Highlight pollution spikes.
D. Location Comparison
* Allow users to compare multiple locations.
* Display AQI and pollutant differences.
* Visualize historical trends.
* Identify locations with relatively better air quality using available measurements.
E. Health & Safety Dashboard
* Explain AQI categories and their meaning.
* Provide general health precautions.
* Display outdoor activity recommendations.
* Highlight precautions for sensitive groups.
* Explain the impact of PM2.5 and PM10.
F. AQI Forecast
* Display forecasted air quality.
* Show expected pollution trends.
* Highlight potential pollution spikes.
* Clearly distinguish forecasts from actual readings.
4. Bonus Features
Extra Points
1. AI AQI Assistant: Chatbot that explains pollution levels and answers AQI-related questions.
2. Smart AQI Alerts: Notify users when AQI crosses user-defined thresholds.
3. ML-Based Prediction: Predict future AQI using historical pollution and weather data.
4. Pollution Heatmap: Visualize pollution intensity across different locations.
5. Location-Based AQI: Detect user location and display nearby air quality.
6. Clean Air Finder: Identify relatively cleaner locations using available monitoring data.
7. AQI Sharing Cards: Generate shareable pollution reports for social media.
8. Pollution Impact Report: Generate automated historical pollution analysis.
9. Pollution Leaderboard: Rank locations based on air quality.
10. Voice Assistant: Allow users to ask questions about air pollution.
11. Anomaly Detection: Detect unusual spikes in pollution measurements.
12. Multi-API Integration: Combine multiple authentic public data sources.
13. AI Insights: Generate understandable summaries of pollution trends.
